The Accounts APIs let your software work with the customers and suppliers you bill and buy from. You can create, read, change, cancel and delete accounts, manage their contacts, addresses, payment methods, billing preferences and notes, and list the orders, invoices, payments and other records that belong to each account. You can also sort accounts into groups, manage communication profiles and email documents such as invoices and account statements. All 77 endpoints are available in all seven Exsited SDKs.

Common tasks

You want to Use
Add a new customer or supplier Create an account with a name and a type of CUSTOMER, SUPPLIER or CUSTOMER_AND_SUPPLIER, inside account
Find accounts by email address or creation date List accounts with a filter such as filter_email_address_contains or filter_created_on_gte
Change a few of an account's details Update an account's information, sending only the fields to change inside account
Add a contact person to an account Add contacts to an account, giving each contact its slot, CONTACT_1 to CONTACT_5, as type
Save a payment method on an account Add a payment method with processor_type, payment_processor and reference. The first method you add becomes the account's default
Close an account from a date, or open it again Cancel an account with an effective_date, then Reactivate an account with the same body when you need it back. Reactivating has not been confirmed to work yet
See the invoices that belong to one account List an account's invoices, page by page
Email an invoice or an account statement to a customer Email a document, naming the document and the recipients in to. This call has not been confirmed to work yet, and its exact body format is not documented yet

Good to know

Purging an account cannot be undone, and emails are real

Purge an account removes the account and its records permanently. If you only want to stop working with an account, use Cancel an account instead. Email a document and Send and log an email send real email to the recipients you name, so use addresses you control when you try them. None of these three calls has been confirmed to work yet.

Paths take the account's id, not its uuid

Every account path names the account by its id, a short code such as W13ZGY. Passing the account's uuid returns 404.

  • Most writes put their fields inside one key. Account writes use account, a single address address, a contact contact, a payment method payment_method and billing preferences billing_preferences. Setting a portal password and sending an email take their fields at the top level, and adding addresses takes a top-level addresses list.
  • A contact is read by its slot and changed by its id. Retrieve a contact by slot takes CONTACT_1 to CONTACT_5; updating, replacing and deleting a contact take its con_ id. A contact created as CONTACT_1 reads back with type billing.
  • Some fields are written one way and read back another. currency and time_zone go in as a code and a name and read back as objects, an address is written with post_code and reads back with postal_code, and a contact's first_name and last_name read back under name.
  • An account with orders, invoices or payments cannot be deleted. Delete an account returns 409 for it, and retrying does not help.
  • Lists come a page at a time. Records come under data with a pagination object. While has_more is true, send pagination.next_cursor as cursor to get the next page.
  • Account groups cannot yet be changed or removed, and communication profiles cannot yet be created. Update an account group and Delete an account group currently return 500, even for a group just created, and Create a communication profile currently returns 500 and creates nothing, so you can change and delete only profiles that already exist. Once these work, an account group can be deleted only when no account uses it, and the default communication profile cannot be deleted.
