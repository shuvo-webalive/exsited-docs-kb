The work and people APIs let your software manage the people and resources you book, the work they do and the time they spend on it. You can manage labours and labour profiles, set their availability and find labours free to book, create tasks with checklists, record timesheets against booked order lines and read milestones. You can also manage employees, designations, teams and time periods, and read your departments. All 69 endpoints are available in all seven Exsited SDKs.

Good to know

Deletes cannot be undone, and some records are locked

Deleting a labour, labour profile, task, timesheet, employee, designation, team or time period cannot be undone; reading it afterwards returns 404. A labour that has been booked on an order cannot be deleted, so use Cancel a labour instead, and a labour profile that a booked labour uses cannot be deleted either. A team with members cannot be deleted until you remove them, and a checklist item whose status is COMPLETED cannot be deleted and returns 409.

Some paths take a code instead of an id

Task note paths take the task's code; a wt_ id returns 404 there, while the task, checklist and update calls take the wt_ id. List a labour's orders takes the labour's code, and List an employee's milestones takes the employee's code.

  • Each record type has its own wrapper key, and lists come a page at a time. Bodies go inside labour, labour_profile, work_task, timesheet, employee, designation, team or time_period, and the single record comes back under the same key. Listings put their records under data; while pagination.has_more is true, send pagination.next_cursor as cursor to get the next page.
  • A timesheet belongs to a booked order line. sale_order_item is the line's ln_ id with the prefix changed to soi_, on a line that books a labour. Send times such as 2026-11-09T09:00:00Z.
  • Cancel and reactivate a labour with an empty JSON object. Cancel sets status to inactive and reactivate sets it to active.
  • Teams and time periods are turned on and off through status. Send active or inactive in an update. A time period's period goes in upper case, such as MONTHLY, and comes back in lower case.
  • Milestones and departments are read only. These areas have no create, update or delete call, and a department listed with a null id cannot be retrieved on its own.
  • Some calls do not work yet. Update labour availability in bulk currently returns 500. Add a note to a task currently returns 409, so no task note can be created and the calls that need one have not been confirmed to work. Create a designation currently returns 404 and leaves no record, so updating and deleting a designation have not been confirmed to work either.
