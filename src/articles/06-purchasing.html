The purchasing APIs let your software handle what you buy from your suppliers. You can create, change, cancel and read purchase orders, record supplier bills as purchase invoices, pay suppliers, and track the credit a supplier owes you through purchase credit notes and refunds. You can also send purchased stock back to a supplier, generate ABA files from purchase payments and set up receive terms. All eighty-nine endpoints are available in all seven Exsited SDKs.

Common tasks

Order goods from a supplier Create a purchase order with the supplier's account_id and its lines, inside purchase_order. Send "draft": true to save it as a draft

Record the supplier's bill for a purchase order Create a purchase invoice from a purchase order with {"purchase_invoice": {}} as the body
Pay one purchase invoice Create a purchase payment for a purchase invoice, sending only payment_method_applications inside purchase_payment
Create a purchase order and its bill and payment in one call Create a purchase order in one call, with the supplier in account.id and the order in account.purchase_order
Record money a supplier pays back Create a purchase refund from a purchase credit note, with the reference of one of the supplier's payment methods as payment_method
Send purchased stock back to a supplier Create a return vendor authorization, naming the purchase order and each line with the rva_qty to return
Build an ABA file from purchase payments Generate an ABA file, listing each payment's id under aba_file.transactions

Good to know

Deletes cannot be undone, so cancel when you can

Deleting a purchase order, purchase invoice, purchase payment, purchase refund or purchase credit note, or one of their notes or files, cannot be undone. If you may need a purchase order or purchase invoice again, cancel it instead: Cancel a purchase order and Cancel a purchase invoice set the status to inactive, and the reactivate calls set it back to active. Only a draft return vendor authorization can be deleted; any other status returns 400 stock_committed.

Overpaying a supplier raises a purchase credit note

A purchase payment that pays more than its invoices take raises a purchase credit note. List a purchase payment's purchase credit notes shows it. Applying a purchase credit note to purchase invoices in turn records a purchase payment.

  • Each record type has its own wrapper key. Write bodies go inside purchase_order, purchase_invoice, purchase_payment, purchase_refund, purchase_credit_note or return_vendor_authorization. The cancel and reactivate calls take an empty object.
  • Purchase order note calls take the purchase order's code, not its id. That covers listing, creating, reading and deleting notes and their files.
  • Some calls take only the prefixed id. The purchase credit note apply, note and delete calls take the pcn_ id, and deleting a return vendor authorization takes the rva_ id. The reads accept either the id or the code.
  • Amounts are decimal strings. Send purchase payment amounts as text, such as "1.00".
  • A purchase refund's amount cannot change. Update a purchase refund takes only alternate_date, reference, note and custom_attributes; any other key returns 400.
  • Lists come a page at a time. Records come under data. While pagination.has_more is true, send pagination.next_cursor as cursor to get the next page.
