The settings APIs let your software read and change how your Exsited instance is set up. You can manage currencies, taxes, warehouses, item groups, variations, payment processors, payment terms, pricing levels, discount and shipping profiles, custom objects, field settings and the general settings groups. You can also manage accounting codes, additional codes, billing, charging and pricing periods, invoice terms, salutations, document and email templates and roles, and read days of the month and countries. All one hundred and forty-one endpoints are available in all seven Exsited SDKs.

Good to know

Updating the general group resets other settings

Update the general group of general settings also sets eight other instance settings, such as accountingPeriod and fiscalYear, to null, and no API call can set them back. Do not send this call unless you can reset those settings in the Exsited web app. Currencies and taxes also stay once you create them: there is no call that deletes either.

General settings are read and written one group at a time

The group is one of account, item, date_and_time, order, purchase, labour, transaction, security, dashboard, portal, display or general. The dashboard and security groups store flags as "1" and "0", so send flags to those groups that way.

  • Each record type has its own wrapper key. A currency goes inside currency, a warehouse inside warehouse, an accounting code inside accounting_code, and general settings inside general_settings. Reads return the record under the same key.
  • Periods link to a time period in two ways. A billing period takes the time period's hyphenated uuid; charging and pricing periods take its tp_ id.
  • Some writes return a different shape from the read. A payment term or invoice term create returns a flat period and duration, where a read nests them under duration.
  • Read a country again after you update it. Update a country returns the country's values from before the change.
  • Default and system records cannot be deleted. The default payment processor, the default pricing level and a role whose is_system_role is true cannot be deleted.
  • Lists come a page at a time. Records come under data. Send limit for the page size, then pass pagination.next_cursor as cursor while pagination.has_more is true.
